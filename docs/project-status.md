## Remaining governance gates

The repository contains design artifacts only. Before a real go-live decision, the team must replace requested and design-only evidence with vendor assurance, configurations, access reviews, logs, restore tests, migration reconciliation, and an exercised incident-response record. Named team members and reviewers are intentionally not invented and remain a team-governance decision.
